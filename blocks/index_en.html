Needs that remained invisible until now.

Managing stray and owned dogs is a statutory duty of municipalities in Romania – yet many reach the limits of their organisational or financial capacity. At the same time, numerous national and international organisations are committed to sustainable solutions: CNVR programmes, shelter placement with adoption promotion, or combinations of both approaches. Their support, however, often remains uncoordinated. CAMRO creates a structured connection between municipal needs and external support – for municipalities committed to humane and lasting solutions.

Statutory duty
The management of stray dogs in Romania is a statutory municipal duty governed by specific legislation.
CNVR & Shelter
Neutering & return or shelter placement with adoption promotion – both approaches are eligible for support.

How a cooperation comes about

  1. 1

    Municipality reports its need

    A municipality sets out its specific needs in managing stray and owned dogs via a standardised online form.

  2. 2

    Verification & assessment

    The CAMRO team reviews the information; an independent local partner verifies it in addition.

  3. 3

    Targeted cooperation

    External organisations provide precisely fitting support – complementary, not as a replacement for municipal responsibility.

What CAMRO is – and what it deliberately is not.

CAMRO is

  • a structuring cooperation platform
  • voluntary and terminable at any time
  • based on verified, transparent needs
  • supplementary support alongside the municipality’s own contribution

CAMRO is not

  • a donation collection platform
  • an automatic funding commitment
  • a replacement for municipal responsibility
  • open to individuals or informal initiatives
